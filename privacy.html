Privacy

The short version:
your data stays put.

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This policy applies to the Bullpen iPhone app and this support website.

Bullpen does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party trackers.

Information you enter

Team names, pitcher names, jersey numbers, throwing hand, roles, notes, session dates, pitch counts, strike counts, and intensity are stored only in the app's local Application Support directory on your device. This information is never sent to us or to any third party.

Network access

Bullpen does not require or make network requests. The app has no server component, no cloud sync, and does not create its own remote backups. If you have iOS device backups enabled, your device's backup settings may include the app's local data; those backups are controlled by iOS and your iCloud preferences, not by Bullpen.

This website

This support site is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. It does not set cookies, run analytics, or include advertising. GitHub may process standard technical logs, such as an IP address and browser type, to serve the site. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete individual sessions from the Sessions screen.
  • Delete a pitcher and that pitcher's session history from the Roster screen.
  • Remove all app data under Team → Data → Delete all data.
  • Uninstall the app, which removes its local container and all stored data.

Children's privacy

Bullpen does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Coaches should follow their school or organization's policies when entering player information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with a new effective date. Because Bullpen does not collect data, future changes are expected to be limited to clarifications.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Bullpen support page. Bullpen is maintained by Dan Carlstedt.
