Need a hand?

Bullpen is local, private, and designed to stay out of the way in the dugout. Here are the answers coaches ask for most.

Availability

How is readiness calculated?

Bullpen combines the pitcher's most recent session with the team recovery model. Aggressive shortens the rest window by one day when the pitcher threw more than 20 pitches. Standard uses the baseline rest table. Conservative adds a day for outings over 15 pitches.

Ready means the rest window is complete and the seven-day total is below the team caution line. Limited means the pitcher already threw today or has reached the caution line. Resting means a rest window is still active; the earliest return date is shown on the pitcher page.

Sessions

Can I edit or delete a session?

Yes. Open Sessions, tap any entry, change the details, and save. The editor also has a delete action. Availability and workload recalculate immediately.

Roster

How do I remove a pitcher?

Open Roster, tap the pitcher, then use the ellipsis menu and choose Remove pitcher. Deleting a pitcher also deletes their session history. If you want to keep the history, edit the pitcher and turn off Active roster instead.

Data

How do I start over?

Open Team → Data → Delete all data. You can also restore the Riverside Rams sample team from the same section. Uninstalling the app removes all local data.

Privacy

Does Bullpen sync between devices?

No. Bullpen stores everything only on the device where it was entered. There is no account, cloud sync, analytics, or tracking. See the privacy policy for details.
